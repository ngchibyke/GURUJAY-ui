# Marketplace Build Roadmap

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-abuja-marketplace-design.md`

The spec covers several independent subsystems, so it is built as a sequence of plans. Each plan ends in working, tested software. Only the plan marked **written** exists so far; each later plan is written once the one before it lands, so it can build on real interfaces instead of guessed ones.

| # | Plan | Delivers | Depends on | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Server foundation** | Rust API: sign-up/sign-in with phone OTP, sessions, roles, access codes, stores (max 2), products and variants, audit log, encrypted personal fields | — | **written** (`2026-10-23-01-server-foundation.md`) |
| 2 | Payments & escrow | Paystack bank-account verification, cart, one payment split into orders per store, append-only ledger, 5% cut, release (handover code / 3 days after delivered), 30-day backstop queue, cancellations, 48h ship deadline, refunds | 1 | not written |
| 3 | Delivery & tracking | FCT-only checkout gate, NIPOST postcode adapter (with manual fallback), Shipbubble rates/booking/webhooks, self-delivery steps, handover codes, 6pm cut-off and promised date, tracking timeline API, phone reveal while out for delivery | 1, 2 | not written |
| 4 | Chat, moderation & reviews | Order/product chat over WebSocket, word filter, Claude Haiku tone check, scam-pattern blocking, one-tap rewrite, strikes and mutes, reviews by confirmed buyers | 1, 2 | not written |
| 5 | Disputes & notifications | Complaint flow with evidence, 48h seller response, owner decisions (full/partial refund, release), FCM push, in-app inbox, confirm-delivery reminders | 2, 3, 4 | not written |
| 6 | Owner web dashboard | Codes, users/stores, orders and ledger, disputes, moderation queue, settings, Owner/Support roles | 1–5 | not written |
| 7 | Flutter app | Buyer and seller screens, including the polished tracking screen; may be split into sub-plans (auth & browse, seller tools, checkout & tracking, chat) | 1–5 | not written |
| 8 | Data rights & retention | Data export, soft-delete and purge jobs, chat retention (12 months), privacy notice content for legal review | 1–5 | not written |

## Owner actions that run in parallel (not code)

- Open Paystack and Shipbubble business accounts. Needed by plans 2 and 3.
- Get legal/compliance review of the escrow setup (CBN) and DPO review of AI chat processing and retention. Needed before launch.
